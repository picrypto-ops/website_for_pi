title: 
About Us

slogan: 


home_description: 
Founded in 1999 by Aron Cohen and Jonny Kaye, the Pi Group has spent over two decades identifying and capitalizing on high-value opportunities across various sectors and asset classes, both in Israel and globally.
Pi's journey began with Aron and Jonny recognizing significant pricing inefficiencies in Israel's early listed derivatives market. By 2001, Pi had grown into one of the country's largest proprietary derivative trading firms.
While proprietary trading and investing have always been at Pi's core, we gradually expanded into other areas of the Israeli financial market. In 2003, working closely with the Israeli SEC, Pi issued Israel's first structured product, the Hadas, raising 600 million ILS. Two years later, in 2005, we launched the Shacharit, Israel's first ETF, raising an additional 600 million ILS.
Pi also played a pioneering role in Israel's hedge fund industry. In 2004, we established Arbitrade, the country's first hedge fund, followed by Pi EMF, a global macro derivatives fund, in 2005. Pi EMF maintained a flawless track record, generating positive returns every year throughout its 16-year lifespan.
More recently, in 2023, we co-founded Pi Pituach, capitalizing on both the growing demand for short-term fixed-income investments and inefficiencies in local market regulations. Since its launch, Pi Pituach has issued over 7.5 billion ILS in bonds for Israeli money market funds.
Through innovation, adaptability, and deep market expertise, Pi continues to seek and seize exceptional investment opportunities.
